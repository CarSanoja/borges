# Live runs on Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory

All five cases ran live on 2 October 2026 with an empty response cache.

| Role | Model |
|---|---|
| Planner, adversary, judge, repairer, reviewer | `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b` |
| Coder | `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B` |
| Narrator | `nvidia/Nemotron-3-Ultra-550b-a55b` |

The recordings the web app plays by default are the event logs in `cases/<case>/recorded-live/events.jsonl`. `borges reproduce --case <case> --source live --universe <id>` re-executes a recorded universe from scratch. The results must match the recording exactly.

How the live numbers compare with outside references:
- **red_cards.** OR 1.34 [1.14, 1.58] is the same default analysis the case cites. The 29 teams of Silberzahn et al. (2018) reported between 0.89 and 2.93.
- **marp_religiosity.** The positive association agrees with the Many-Analysts Religion Project.
- **checkout_ab.** The data is synthetic, generated by `cases/checkout_ab/generate.py`. No plan was scripted: Nemotron found the planted internal-traffic artefact on its own.

## What went wrong before these runs, and what changed

The first live batches failed in ways the scripted demos could not show. Each failure was fixed in the code, with a test, before the next batch.

## Cost

The development day made 45 live runs, 31 of which completed. Their recorded cost was $0.65. The spend ledger (`.borges/spend.json`) also counts the smoke checks: $0.76 over 1,096 calls. Every run that was not committed was rejected after a hand review, and the reasons are in the table above or in the commits that fixed them.

A complete live run of one case costs between $0.015 and $0.033.
